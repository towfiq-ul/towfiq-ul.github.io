Privacy Policy

Investment (Android app, package com.advancedinventorybd.investment)
Effective date: 13 November 2026

In short: your investment records stay on your phone. If you turn on backup, a copy is saved to your own Google Drive. The developer runs no servers, receives none of your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

This policy explains what data the Investment app ("the app", "we") handles, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. It applies only to this app.

1. Data the app handles

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Google account name, email address and profile photo (from Google Sign-In) To show who is signed in and to connect to your Google Drive for backups On your phone only
Investment records you enter or import: investment names, partner names, contribution amounts, returns, dates, notes The core function of the app: tracking investments and splitting returns On your phone (local database); in your Google Drive if backup is on
A spreadsheet you choose to import (an .xlsx file, or a Google Sheet picked through Android's file picker) To copy your existing records into the app Read on your phone; the app does not upload it anywhere
App settings (backup on/off, app lock on/off, review-prompt dates) To remember your choices On your phone only

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, messages, call logs, device identifiers, advertising ID, or browsing data. It does not ask for any Android permission besides internet access.

2. Google Drive backup

When you turn on backup, the app saves a single file, investment-backup.json, to your Google Drive and can restore it later. The app uses the limited drive.file permission, which only lets it see files it created itself. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. The backup is stored in your Google account under Google's own terms; the developer has no access to it.

3. Fingerprint / app lock

If you turn on the app lock, unlocking is handled entirely by Android's system biometric prompt. The app never receives, stores or sends your fingerprint, face data or screen-lock PIN; it only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Data leaves your phone only in these cases, all started by you and all sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections:

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive (Google LLC): to sign you in and to save or restore your backup. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play in-app review: if you choose to rate the app, the review is handled by Google Play, not by the app.

The developer operates no servers and receives no copy of your data.

5. Google API Services – Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the sign-in and backup features described above, is not transferred to anyone else, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

6. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your records are only copied off the phone through the Drive backup you control. All network traffic uses HTTPS. You can add a fingerprint lock in Profile & settings.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your phone: kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of it.
  • Drive backup: delete investment-backup.json from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Google access: sign out in the app, and/or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Because the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children

The app is a financial record-keeping tool meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
